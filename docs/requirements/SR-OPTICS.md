# SR-OPTICS — calibration & optical preset requirements

Serves: **O1** (standardized acquisition), **O3** (accuracy), **O4** (standalone
app). Consumed by BENCH, DATASET and STUDY.

A preset is **hardware**, not project data. The existing code already treats the
`calibration` fact as global rather than per-study — this surface makes that
explicit and safe.

| `SR-OPTICS-031` | The surface **shall** let a stored calibration be **loaded** the way a preset is: a `Load...` that lists the **current device's** calibrations, newest first, **marking the one in force**, and loading one **makes it the calibration in force** *and* shows its proof photo. | `SR-OPTICS-033` | Where a distortion model is measured, the surface **shall** store it with the preset **only** when its unwarped residual passes discorpy's own sub-pixel rule (a residual over 1 px on more than 15 % of the points is not good enough), and **shall** carry the residual with the record. | Measured 2026-10-19: the same flow reaches 0.264 px rms on a single-weight synthetic grid but only 7.87 px (worst line 20.03 px) on printed graph paper, whose three line weights (1/5/10 mm) break the one-uniform-family assumption. | `SR-OPTICS-034` | When frames are corrected, the corrected frame **shall** be treated as a **different image**: its scale **shall** be re-measured from the corrected reference and recorded, and the model (centre, coefficients, residual) **shall** travel with the measurement that used it. | `c0` is the centre magnification, not a scale - a corrected frame's `px_per_um` differs from the uncorrected one, and a number whose scale cannot be traced to the correction that produced it is the provenance gap `projectbrief.md` non-negotiable 2 exists to prevent. | `T` | proposed | O1, O3 |
| `SR-OPTICS-035` | The surface **shall** measure the scale from a photo **as this rig produces it** - grid paper seen through an eyepiece: a circular field stop that may be cut off by the frame, a pale print whose every fifth line is heavier, a lattice tilted by several degrees. | Measured 2026-10-19: `vision/grid.py` refuses **all nine** photos in `recordings/calibration` (its prominence gate sees the field stop's edge, its period search lands on a harmonic). A surface with no scale cannot measure anything, so "the operator clicks a span by hand" was the only route left - and it is the route whose accuracy `SR-OPTICS-005` has to warn about. | `T` `tests/test_lattice.py` | implemented | O1, O3 |
| `SR-OPTICS-036` | The surface **shall** report the **measured drift** of the scale across the field (the spread of the local readings that produced it) as a number **separate** from the scale's uncertainty, and **shall** state that a drift it could not measure is unmeasured rather than zero. | The two are different facts: the uncertainty is how well the centre was measured, the drift is how much the optics change it elsewhere. Measured on this rig's photo: 27.24 px per cell (from 10 bands) with a **5.1 %** spread across a 40 mm field - 114 µm on a 2 mm animal, seven times the print's own tolerance. Reporting one number for both is what made "keep specimens central" advice nobody could check. | `T` `tests/test_lattice.py` | implemented | O3 |

## Open issues

- `SR-OPTICS-006` is the gating requirement for "measure much smaller
  organisms": it needs a data-model decision (a new `optical_setups` table, or
  extend `calibration_profiles` with an objective identity + drop `is_active` in
  favour of a selected setup). The Calibrate screen's **Load...** made *switching*
  possible on the current single-active-row model (2026-10-20); the data model is
  what is still open.
- `SR-OPTICS-009`: the accuracy target is currently a literature constant; confirm
  the threshold with the supervisor before enforcing it.
- `SR-OPTICS-033` needs a decision about the **target**, and it is a lab decision, not
  a code one: printed graph paper is what the field users have, and it is the *wrong*
  target for a distortion model (three line weights). A **single-weight line grid** or
  a **dot grid** at the specimen plane makes the method work - measured, not hoped
  (`docs/calibration.md` §3 has the oracle and the photo result side by side). Which
  sheet the lab prints is the open question.
- **Independent referee, not yet used:** *ImageJ / FIJI* (`Set Scale` + *Lens
  Distortion Correction*) is the gold-standard way to check the app's pixel->micron
  readings from outside this codebase. Nobody has done it yet; it is the cheapest
  available answer to "is the scale right?" that does not trust our own code.
